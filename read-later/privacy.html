Read Later privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Read Later is a browser extension that saves your place on a web page so you can return to it later. This policy describes what the extension stores and what it does not do.

Data stored on your device

When you mark a page, the extension saves the following in Chrome’s local storage on that device:

  • The page address and title
  • The page’s favicon address
  • The scroll position, page height, and an approximate reading percentage
  • A short excerpt of about 80 characters from the text near the top of the screen, used to find the same paragraph later
  • The time you saved the mark

This data stays in the browser profile where you installed the extension. Read Later does not run a server, and it does not transmit this data to the developer or to any third party.

How the data is used

The saved information is used only to show your reading list and to reopen a page at the marked position. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to assess credit or for lending.

Deleting data

You can delete a single entry from the extension popup. Uninstalling the extension removes the data stored by the extension in that browser profile.
